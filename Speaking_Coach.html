A. モデルメール

💼 Camden 価格改定メール（Day 12 Writing モデル版）

I'm writing to propose a pricing change for our lunch set at the Camden flagship.
Here's the context: office workers are price-sensitive on weekdays, so we launched a £12 lunch set —
but the number of customers isn't rising, and lunch sets show higher elasticity than dinner mains.
Rather than cut the £12 set to a cheaper price, we will introduce a £16 premium anchor
so that customers trade up and AOV rises above £14.
Three weeks of testing will show whether AOV rises from £14 toward £15.50.
Premium pricing is only as strong as the brand equity behind it.
I look forward to hearing from you. 🔗 look-forward / hearing-from

Our premium flagship drives margin even at lower volume.
Dynamic pricing on weekday lunches lifts off-peak AOV.
3× シャドーイング:

② 母音の長短 🟡

Minimal pairs: peak / pick  ·  sheep / ship  ·  store / stir  ·  seat / sit  ·  three / thri
We need a more efficient kitchen workflow during peak hours. (peak ≠ pick)
We will open three new stores this year. (long iː / ɔː)
3× シャドーイング:

③ TH 🟢

voiceless θ: three · thoughts · through  |  voiced ð: the · this · that
I'd like to share my thoughts on the brand strategy.
舌先を上の歯に軽く当てる。"three" ≠ "tree" · "thoughts" ≠ "sorts"

could→you (/dʒ/化) · stores→in · peak→hours · value→proposition · look→forward
Could you walk me through the value proposition for this market?
could-you / walk-me / value-proposition を繋いで言う

D. Day別テーマスクリプト

Day 17 — Value & Vendor Strategy

ターゲット: ④ V / B 🔵 ＋ 🔗 linking ／ V＝上の歯を下唇に軽く当てて有声で「ヴ」（摩擦）／B＝両唇を閉じて破裂。語頭の v→b 化に注意
Minimal pairs（V vs B）: van / ban  ·  vest / best  ·  vote / boat  ·  vase / base  ·  curve / curb  ·  very / berry
1We need a vendor who can deliver high volume at a viable price.
2The beverage brand brings better margins than our base menu.
3Before we sign, let's verify that the budget covers both brands.
4Our value proposition must be visible to every busy buyer.
5A reliable vendor base is more profitable across the volume curve.
弱点メモ: 新規ターゲット。語頭の v→b 化（vendor→"bendor" / value→"balue"）と、語中・語末の v（deliver / covers / curve）が弱点になりやすい。both brands / base is = linking 継続。Day 17 実施後に所見を追記。
3× シャドーイング:

Day 16 — New Store Opening / Unit Economics

ターゲット: ① R / L 再 🔴 ＋ 🔗 linking ／ R/L の再ローテ（定着確認回）。R＝舌先フリー＋唇丸め／L＝舌先を上歯茎にタッチ＋唇リラックス
Minimal pairs（glue 系中心）: grew / glue  ·  rate / late  ·  fry / fly  ·  crew / clue  ·  road / load
1We're planning to open three new stores along the river in east London.
2The rollout speed depends on how fast we train the kitchen crew.
3Our flagship location grew faster than projected.
4Labour costs stay flat when the floor is run by an experienced manager.
弱点メモ: Day 13 の glue→"girl" 化は Day 16 再ローテで定着確認済み（再発なし）。floor is = Linking R 継続。
3× シャドーイング:

Day 15 — Brand Positioning / Localization

ターゲット: ③ TH 🟢 ＋ 🔗 linking ／ voiceless θ（three / thoughts / through）と voiced ð（the / this / that / with）を作り分ける。舌先を上の歯に軽く当てる
1Three things stay fixed in every market: the promise, the flagship, the service standard.
2I'd like your thoughts on whether this local edge fits the brand.
3Walk me through the plan for the Thai launch.
4That's the core; everything else can flex with local taste.
5With a clear playbook, the brand can thrive in both markets.
弱点メモ: ð → D/Z 化注意（the→"duh", that's→"dats"）。連結部 with-local / in-both で ð が次語に潰れやすい。単語単独では θ/ð とも産出可（Day 15 所見）＝連結 speech が課題の中心。
3× シャドーイング:

Day 14 — Customer Experience / Service

ターゲット: ② 母音長 iː 🟡 ＋ 🔗 linking ／ 長 iː（黄）と短 ɪ（脱力）を作り分ける
1We seat guests fast, but we never let service feel rushed.
2A quick visit can still leave a strong impression if the greeting is warm.
3We repeat the same standard at peak hours: greet, seat, serve.
4Listen first, fix the issue, then check in again.
5Consistency keeps guests coming back — every shift, every visit.
弱点メモ: seat / sit の短 ɪ に力が入りすぎる（→ iː に化ける）。短い語ほど脱力。文4は短 ɪ 連続の難所。
3× シャドーイング:

Day 13 — Store Ops / Kitchen Workflow

ターゲット: ① R / L 🔴 ＋ 🔗 linking
1During peak hours, the kitchen workflow needs to be faster.
2We redrew the prep line at the flagship store in Camden.
3Clear roles and a simple playbook keep the floor under control.
4Our servers learned to clear tables faster without rushing customers.
5A great lunch rush rewards a well-drilled team.
弱点メモ: glue が "girl" に化ける（"loo→glue" ルート）／ floor under = Linking R。
